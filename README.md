# Black & White 2 Terastallization Hack
A hack to add terastallization to Black and White 2
Features:
The Tera type byte, stored in 0x86 of the pokemon data
One Terastallization per side per battle
The Tera button (tap it or press L)
Important trainers once you get the orb use the mechanic
You get the orb from Colress during the first encounter
You can change your tera type from the business man in the pokecenter in Castelia City

Patching: you need to use xdelta and a black 2/white 2 NDSi Enhanced rom.
